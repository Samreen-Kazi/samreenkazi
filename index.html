About Me

I have always been comfortable stepping into the unknown—exploring new challenges, taking calculated risks, and learning through experience. Over time, I realized that the best way to face uncertainty is through preparation and understanding the risks ahead. That mindset naturally led me to cybersecurity.

I am a cybersecurity professional focused on Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC), Identity & Access Management (IAM), and Security Operations. I enjoy solving security challenges from both a governance and technical perspective—building policies, assessing risks, identifying vulnerabilities, and helping organizations create stronger security foundations.

Through my internship experiences, I have worked with startups across different regions, collaborating with founders and development teams to improve security practices. My work has included developing security policies, conducting compliance assessments, performing vulnerability testing, supporting remediation efforts, and strengthening security awareness.

My goal is to help organizations prepare for the unexpected by building secure, resilient, and practical security solutions.
